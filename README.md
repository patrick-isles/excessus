# EXCESSUS

> *Assenting up to the point of collapse.*

A procedural audio-visual limit-experience engine built around the threshold where ecstasy, terror, and dissolution become indistinguishable.

Using real-time **Web Audio API** synthesis and a generative **Chiaroscuro Canvas2D** renderer—with zero audio samples and zero dependencies—`excessus` models the gradual surrender of bounded form into overwhelming continuity. Bowed sub-harmonic strings and dark brass swells corrode through a saturating feedback delay network, sculpted noise walls, and cavernous percussive strikes as the participant deliberately pushes the system past structural stability.

**Live Experience:** https://patrick-isles.github.io/excessus/

---

## Four Phases of Expenditure

| Phase | Threshold | State |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **I · DISCONTINUITAS** | `0.00 – 0.30` | Fragile bowed sub-strings, cavernous stone reverb, slow irregular timpani pulse |
| **II · EXALTATIO** | `0.30 – 0.65` | Rising microtonal harmonics, swelling feedback delay network, trembling vessel boundary |
| **III · EXCESSUS** | `0.65 – 0.92` | Corrosive noise wall, asymmetric waveshaping saturation, arterial crimson bleed & horizontal tearing |
| **IV · DISSOLUTIO** | `0.92 – 1.00` | Sovereign collapse: full harmonic saturation and particle dispersion before an abrupt cut back into void |

---

## Interaction

- **Hold `Space` or `[ HOLD TO ASSENT ]`** to drive the system continuously toward the sovereign collapse point
- **Sliders:** `THRESHOLD`, `FEEDBACK`, `CORROSION`
- **Controls:** `LOCK` (hold current tension without gravitational recoil), `VOID` (`R` — cut back to stillness), `SILENCE`, and `H` (hide interface)
